# DevToolBox Sponsorship Media Kit

DevToolBox is a free online developer tools site for people working with JSON, APIs, encoders, formatters, generators, security utilities, DevOps snippets, CSS tools, and technical guides.

## Audience Fit

- Developers solving implementation tasks in-session.
- Search visitors with tool-specific intent.
- Buyers and evaluators of developer platforms, APIs, hosting, IDEs, databases, security tools, productivity tools, and technical education products.

## Sponsorship Packages

### Category Sponsor

Own a high-intent category such as JSON tools, CSS tools, security tools, DevOps tools, converter tools, or web tools.

Inventory can include:

- Category page sponsor CTA.
- Relevant tool sidebar CTA.
- Selected tool-page partner placement.

Suggested starter budget: US$299-US$799 per month.

### Article Sponsor

Promote a product inside developer guides and comparison articles.

Inventory can include:

- Article sponsor CTA.
- Related tool block.
- Optional blog-list presence.

Suggested starter budget: US$149-US$499 per article.

### Partner Test

Run a small campaign before committing to a larger buy.

Inventory can include:

- One to three tracked placements selected by category and traffic fit.
- Short test window.
- Post-test placement recommendation.

Suggested starter budget: US$99-US$299 per test.

## Budget Notes

Starter budgets are guidance for low-friction tests, not fixed rate-card guarantees. Final pricing depends on target category, placement mix, campaign length, traffic fit, and whether the sponsor needs custom copy or reporting.

## Measurement

Sponsor impressions and clicks are tracked by source, category, package, and placement. Current tracking events include:

- `monetization_impression`
- `monetization_click`
- `newsletter_signup`
- `tool_share`
- `content_feedback`
- `tool_search_no_results`

## Creative Requirements

Recommended assets:

- Product name and landing page URL.
- One short value proposition.
- Preferred category or topic fit.
- Optional logo or brand color.
- Target country or language preference.
- Budget range and campaign dates.
